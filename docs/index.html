🐾 Paw Rivals

Paw Rivals publishes short animated cat vs dog videos: the same everyday situation, two very different reactions. All characters, rooms, music and sound effects are drawn and generated by our own code.

This site describes the publishing tool behind the Paw Rivals YouTube channel. The tool renders our own original animations and uploads them to our own channel using the YouTube Data API. It does not access any other user's data.
